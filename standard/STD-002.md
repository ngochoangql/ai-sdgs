# STD-002 — COMPLIANCE, RISK & EXCEPTION GOVERNANCE

## 1. PURPOSE & GOVERNANCE SCOPE

`STD-002` defines how compliance states are evaluated, how engineering and AI execution risks are classified, and how temporary or scoped deviations (`EXC-*` waivers and intentional technical debt) are governed without eroding baseline integrity.

This standard governs `.aisdgs/adoption-profile.yaml`, `governance/EXCEPTIONS.md`, risk evaluation inside Change Requests, and compliance exit-code semantics across local and CI environments.

---

## 2. TERMS & NORMATIVE DEFINITIONS

- **Compliance State:** Deterministic classification of a repository's adherence to its declared Adoption Tier (`COMPLIANT`, `COMPLIANT_WITH_WAIVERS`, `NON_COMPLIANT`, or `INTEGRITY_COMPROMISED`).
- **Compensating Control:** An alternative safeguard put in place during an active `EXC-*` waiver to bound residual risk until full compliance is restored.
- **Silent Deviation:** Any departure from an applicable `MUST` or `SHOULD` requirement that lacks an active, approved `EXC-*` record or justification.

---

## 3. ROLES & AUTHORITY BOUNDARIES

- **`HUM-OWNER`:** Approves `LOW` and `MEDIUM` risk waivers in `LITE` and `STANDARD` tiers; co-approves `HIGH` risk waivers.
- **`HUM-ARCH`:** Evaluates architectural/technical debt waivers and compensating design controls.
- **`HUM-SEC`:** Holds mandatory veto and sign-off authority over any waiver touching security, privacy, authentication, secrets, dependencies, or CI enforcement (`HIGH` / `CRITICAL` risk).
- **`AI-ANALYST` / `AI-STEWARD`:** MAY assess risk levels, surface expiring waivers, and draft `DRAFT` exception proposals; MUST NOT approve, extend, or delete exception records.

---

## 4. NORMATIVE REQUIREMENTS

- **`STD-002-R01` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Explicit Requirement Applicability Classification:** Every adopting project MUST maintain `.aisdgs/adoption-profile.yaml` classifying standards and conditional capabilities as `MANDATORY`, `CONDITIONAL_ACTIVE`, `OPTIONAL_ENABLED`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Marking any tier-default requirement as `NOT_APPLICABLE` MUST include a written justification and `HUM-OWNER` approval.
- **`STD-002-R02` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Four-Level Risk Classification Matrix:** Every Change Request (`CR-FULL`), architectural exception, and security review MUST classify risk into one of four canonical levels based on blast radius and reversibility:
  - **`LOW`:** Localized, easily reversible, zero contract/schema/security impact.
  - **`MEDIUM`:** Cross-module logic, internal contract updates, or minor migration with tested rollback.
  - **`HIGH`:** Public API breaking change, destructive DB migration, new external runtime dependency, or auth/permission boundary touch.
  - **`CRITICAL`:** Core cryptographic/security control mutation, production data-loss potential, constitutional boundary touch, or emergency production bypass.
- **`STD-002-R03` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Prohibition of Silent Deviations:** AI agents and human contributors MUST NOT bypass, disable, or ignore applicable governance rules silently. Any permissible deviation from a `[WAIVABLE]` rule MUST be recorded as an approved `EXC-XXXX` entry in `governance/EXCEPTIONS.md` (or `.aisdgs/adoption-profile.yaml` waivers list) prior to merge.
- **`STD-002-R04` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Non-Waivable Rule Enforcement:** No `EXC-*` waiver MAY target any requirement tagged `[NON-WAIVABLE]` in `STD-001`..`STD-012`, nor MAY a waiver accept `CRITICAL` unmitigated security exposure (such as committing plaintext credentials). Validators MUST reject such waivers automatically.
- **`STD-002-R05` `[WAIVABLE]` — Mandatory Exception Record Attributes:** Every `EXC-XXXX` record MUST specify: (a) unique ID `EXC-[0-9]{4}`, (b) target rule ID(s) `STD-XXX-Rnn` or project rule ID, (c) affected scope/paths, (d) risk level (`LOW`/`MEDIUM`/`HIGH`), (e) business/technical justification, (f) compensating control, (g) remediation plan, (h) explicit `expires_at` ISO-8601 date, and (i) approving `HUM-*` identity and date.
- **`STD-002-R06` `[WAIVABLE]` — Time-Bounded Waiver Expiration:** Waivers MUST NOT be indefinite by default. Maximum single-grant validity periods MUST NOT exceed **180 days** in `LITE`, **90 days** in `STANDARD`, and **60 days** in `STRICT`. Expired waivers MUST immediately revert to active compliance errors (`Exit Code 1`) until remediated or explicitly renewed by Human Authority.
- **`STD-002-R07` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Deterministic Compliance Exit Semantics:** Validation tooling (`aisdgs validate`) MUST report one of four canonical repository states with deterministic exit codes:
  - `COMPLIANT` (`Exit Code 0`): Zero `MUST` violations; zero expired waivers.
  - `COMPLIANT_WITH_WAIVERS` (`Exit Code 0`): Zero unwaived `MUST` violations; one or more active, unexpired `[WAIVABLE]` exceptions recorded and disclosed in output.
  - `NON_COMPLIANT` (`Exit Code 1`): One or more unwaived `MUST` violations (or `SHOULD` violations under `--strict`), invalid schemas, broken links, or expired waivers.
  - `INTEGRITY_COMPROMISED` (`Exit Code 3`): Tampered `.aisdgs/standard-lock.yaml`, corrupted baseline hash without active CR, or suspected local self-approval spoofing.
- **`STD-002-R08` `[WAIVABLE]` — Technical Debt Registration:** When an AI agent or developer implements a deliberate tactical workaround under time constraints, the workaround MUST reference either an active `EXC-*` waiver or a tracked remediation item in `changes/CHANGELOG-GOVERNANCE.md` rather than leaving undocumented inline hacks.

## 5. OPERATIONAL PROCEDURES & WORKFLOWS

### 5.1 Requesting and Approving a Governance Waiver (`EXC-*`)
1. **Identify Rule & Check Waivability:** Confirm target rule `STD-XXX-Rnn` is tagged `[WAIVABLE]`. If `[NON-WAIVABLE]`, halt immediately (`REJECTED_OR_BLOCKED`).
2. **Draft Exception Proposal (`AI-ANALYST` or Developer):** Populate all 9 attributes required by `STD-002-R05` with `status: DRAFT`, including concrete compensating controls and `expires_at` within tier limits.
3. **Human Risk Review:** Route to `HUM-OWNER` (`LOW`/`MEDIUM`) or `HUM-ARCH` + `HUM-SEC` (`HIGH` or `STRICT` tier).
4. **Activate & Verify:** Upon human approval, set `status: APPROVED`, run `aisdgs validate` to confirm transition to `COMPLIANT_WITH_WAIVERS`, and commit alongside `PROJECT-BASELINE.md` update.

### 5.2 Waiver Expiration & Remediation Loop
1. `aisdgs status` and `aisdgs report` surface a warning banner for any `EXC-*` expiring within 14 days.
2. Before `expires_at`, the team either completes the remediation plan (closing `EXC-*` as `CLOSED`) or conducts a fresh risk review to issue a bounded renewal.
3. Once `current_date > expires_at`, `aisdgs validate` fails (`Exit Code 1`) blocking new merges in gated tiers until resolved.

## 6. PROPORTIONAL TIER SCALING RULES

| Dimension | `TIER-1: LITE` | `TIER-2: STANDARD` | `TIER-3: STRICT` |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Max Waiver Duration (`R06`) | ≤ 180 days | ≤ 90 days | ≤ 60 days |
| Waiver Storage | Compact table/YAML block in `governance/EXCEPTIONS.md` (created on first waiver) | Mandatory `governance/EXCEPTIONS.md` schema-validated | Mandatory `governance/EXCEPTIONS.md` + linked `RISK-*` register |
| Waiver Approval Gate | `HUM-OWNER` | `HUM-OWNER` (`LOW`/`MED`); `HUM-ARCH`/`HUM-SEC` (`HIGH`) | Dual sign-off (`HUM-ARCH` + `HUM-SEC`) for all waivers |
| `SHOULD` Violation Handling | Advisory warning (`Exit 0`) | Warning locally; `--strict` recommended on CI | Treated as `ERROR` (`Exit 1`) unless justified in `adoption-profile.yaml` |

---

## 7. EXCEPTIONS & ESCALATION RULES

- Rules `STD-002-R01` through `STD-002-R04` and `STD-002-R07` are **`[NON-WAIVABLE]`** (meta-governance rules cannot waive themselves).
- If a project accumulates more than 5 concurrent active `HIGH`-risk waivers, `aisdgs report` MUST flag `GOVERNANCE_EROSION_RISK` for `HUM-OWNER` review.

| Rule ID | `L1-Advisory` | `L2-Deterministic` | `L3-Semantic-Audit` |

---

## 9. VERIFICATION CRITERIA & REQUIRED EVIDENCE

Compliance with `STD-002` is verified when:
1. `.aisdgs/adoption-profile.yaml` passes schema validation and contains non-empty justifications for every `NOT_APPLICABLE` entry.
2. Every active `EXC-*` record references a valid `[WAIVABLE]` rule ID, includes a future `expires_at` within tier limits, and names a valid `HUM-*` approver.
3. `aisdgs validate` produces repeatable, deterministic exit codes matching `STD-002-R07`.
