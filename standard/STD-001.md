# STD-001 — GOVERNANCE CONSTITUTION, HIERARCHY & AUTHORITY MODEL

## 1. PURPOSE & GOVERNANCE SCOPE

`STD-001` establishes the supreme constitutional rules, three-level governance hierarchy, authority boundaries between human stakeholders and AI agents, and normative conflict-precedence laws for any software project governed by **AI-SDGS**.

This standard applies unconditionally to all AI-SDGS Adoption Tiers (`LITE`, `STANDARD`, `STRICT`), onboarding pathways (`GREENFIELD`, `BROWNFIELD`, `ITERATIVE`), AI coding tools, and repository contributors.

---

## 2. TERMS & NORMATIVE DEFINITIONS

Normative requirement keywords (`MUST`, `MUST NOT`, `SHOULD`, `SHOULD NOT`, `MAY`) follow IETF RFC 2119 and RFC 8174 (`DOC-ARCH-002`). Standard-specific terms:

- **Constitutional Invariant:** A foundational governance requirement tagged `[NON-WAIVABLE]` that cannot be overridden or exempted by project rules or `EXC-*` waivers.
- **Role Collapse:** Consolidation of multiple formal Human Authority Roles onto fewer individuals (down to a single human in solo projects) without transferring any human authority to an AI system.
- **Self-Escalation:** Any attempt by an AI agent to expand its authorized file scope, grant itself execution approval, mutate its own governance constraints, or impersonate a Human Authority Role.

---

## 3. ROLES & AUTHORITY BOUNDARIES

### 3.1 Human Authority Roles
Every governed project MUST designate human accountability across four authority domains (which MAY collapse onto one individual in `LITE` or small `STANDARD` teams):
1. **`HUM-OWNER` (Project Owner):** Holds supreme project governance authority, Adoption Tier selection, baseline approval, and unassigned role fallback.
2. **`HUM-PROD` (Product Authority):** Owns functional scope, `REQ-FUNC-*` approval, and business acceptance criteria.
3. **`HUM-ARCH` (Architecture Authority):** Owns `ADR-*` sign-off, module topology, external dependencies, and data/API contracts.
4. **`HUM-SEC` (Security & Operations Authority):** Owns `REQ-SEC-*`, secrets/access boundaries, CI/CD enforcement gates, and emergency change reconciliation.

### 3.2 AI Logical Execution Profiles
AI systems operate strictly as bounded execution instruments under four logical profiles (`AI-ANALYST`, `AI-IMPL`, `AI-VERIFY`, `AI-STEWARD`), separating **Analysis Authority** (inspecting, classifying, impact-analyzing, drafting `DRAFT` proposals) from **Execution Authority** (modifying code, contracts, dependencies, or locked baselines).

---

## 4. NORMATIVE REQUIREMENTS

- **`STD-001-R01` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Prompt Is Not Baseline Authorization:** An AI agent MUST treat every natural-language user prompt as an input signal requiring classification and boundary evaluation, and MUST NOT treat a prompt alone as automatic authorization to mutate an approved project baseline or violate constitutional rules.
- **`STD-001-R02` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Exclusive Human Final Authority:** Final approval authority for `PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md`, `PROJECT-BASELINE.md` locks, `CR-*` approvals requiring human sign-off, `ADR-*` adoptions, and `EXC-*` waivers MUST reside exclusively with designated `HUM-*` authorities. An AI agent MUST NOT assume, simulate, or inherit a `HUM-*` role under any circumstance, including solo-developer projects.
- **`STD-001-R03` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Prohibition of AI Self-Escalation:** An AI agent MUST NOT modify `.aisdgs/standard/**`, `.aisdgs/standard-lock.yaml`, its own permission deny-globs, or transition any artifact's `status` / `approval_status` to `APPROVED` without verifiable human authorization.
- **`STD-001-R04` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Three-Level Separation & Precedence:** Every adopting project MUST preserve separation across Level 1 (`AI-SDGS Core` immutable snapshot), Level 2 (`Project Governance Package`), and Level 3 (`Execution Adapters & CLI Enforcement`). When conflicts arise:
  1. Level 1 `[NON-WAIVABLE]` rules MUST prevail unconditionally.
  2. Level 2 `PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md` MAY tighten Level 1 rules (`SHOULD` → `MUST` or narrower scopes) without a waiver.
  3. Level 2 rules MUST NOT weaken a Level 1 `[WAIVABLE]` `MUST` or `SHOULD` unless backed by an active, unexpired `EXC-*` waiver (`STD-002`).
  4. Level 1 and Level 2 sources MUST prevail over derived Level 3 adapter files (`CLAUDE.md`, `GEMINI.md`, `.cursor/rules/*`).
- **`STD-001-R05` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Local Standard Immutability:** Adopting projects MUST reference a pinned, read-only local snapshot of the universal standard verified by SHA-256 digests in `.aisdgs/standard-lock.yaml`, and MUST NOT edit files inside `.aisdgs/standard/` or `.aisdgs/schemas/` locally.
- **`STD-001-R06` `[WAIVABLE]` — Explicit Project Constitution & Role Assignment:** Every governed repository MUST maintain `governance/PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md` and `.aisdgs/config.yaml` explicitly declaring the project identity, Adoption Tier, Onboarding Pathway, and named human holder(s) of `HUM-OWNER`, `HUM-PROD`, `HUM-ARCH`, and `HUM-SEC` (or explicit Role Collapse to `HUM-OWNER`).
- **`STD-001-R07` `[NON-WAIVABLE]` — Analysis vs. Execution Separation:** AI agents MUST be permitted to analyze impacts, identify governance conflicts, and draft `DRAFT` governance proposals within read-authorized scopes, while mutating source code, contracts, dependencies, or baselines MUST block until required Execution Authority is satisfied.
- **`STD-001-R08` `[WAIVABLE]` — Mandatory Conflict Reporting Before Action:** When a user instruction conflicts with `PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md`, an active `ADR-*`, a locked `REQ-*`, or an applicable `STD-*` rule, the AI agent MUST halt mutation, cite the exact conflicting rule/artifact ID, explain the impact, and present compliant resolution options (e.g., filing a `CR-*` or `EXC-*`).

## 5. OPERATIONAL PROCEDURES & WORKFLOWS

### 5.1 Project Constitutional Establishment
1. Initialize `.aisdgs/config.yaml` and `.aisdgs/standard-lock.yaml` via `aisdgs init`.
2. Author `governance/PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md` specifying project mission, non-negotiable technical/security invariants, protected path globs, and `HUM-*` role mappings.
3. Validate constitutional conformance against Level 1 invariants via `aisdgs validate`.
4. Record `HUM-OWNER` approval and include `PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md` SHA-256 digest in `governance/PROJECT-BASELINE.md`.

### 5.2 Governance Conflict Resolution Protocol
When a rule conflict is detected during prompt handling or validation:
1. Identify the competing rules and their Governance Levels (Level 1 vs. Level 2 vs. Level 3).
2. Apply `STD-001-R04` precedence deterministically.
3. If the requested action violates a `[NON-WAIVABLE]` rule, reject execution (`REJECTED_OR_BLOCKED`).
4. If the requested action conflicts with a Level 2 baseline artifact or `[WAIVABLE]` rule, offer to draft a `CR-*` (`STD-006`) or `EXC-*` (`STD-002`) for human decision.

## 6. PROPORTIONAL TIER SCALING RULES

| Requirement | `TIER-1: LITE` | `TIER-2: STANDARD` | `TIER-3: STRICT` |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `[NON-WAIVABLE]` Rules (`R01`–`R05`, `R07`) | Mandatory (`MUST`) | Mandatory (`MUST`) | Mandatory (`MUST`) |
| `HUM-*` Role Mapping (`R06`) | Single `HUM-OWNER` collapse permitted | Named `HUM-OWNER` + `HUM-ARCH` recommended | Distinct or explicitly ratified `HUM-OWNER`, `HUM-PROD`, `HUM-ARCH`, `HUM-SEC` **mandatory** |
| Constitutional Sign-Off Evidence | Committed `PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md` + baseline hash | Committed + PR/CLI baseline lock verification | Multi-role PR approval + protected branch CI enforcement |

---

## 7. EXCEPTIONS & ESCALATION RULES

- Rules `STD-001-R01` through `STD-001-R05` and `STD-001-R07` are **`[NON-WAIVABLE]`**; any `EXC-*` attempting to waive them MUST be rejected by `aisdgs validate` with `Exit Code 1`.
- Waivers to `STD-001-R06` or `STD-001-R08` (e.g., temporary bootstrap state during Brownfield migration) require `HUM-OWNER` approval under `STD-002` with a maximum duration of 30 days.
- Deadlocks between `HUM-PROD` and `HUM-ARCH` escalate to `HUM-OWNER`; security boundary objections by `HUM-SEC` in `STRICT` tier cannot be overridden without explicit risk acceptance co-signed by `HUM-OWNER` and `HUM-SEC`.

## 9. VERIFICATION CRITERIA & REQUIRED EVIDENCE

A project is compliant with `STD-001` when:
1. `.aisdgs/config.yaml` and `governance/PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md` exist, validate against schema, and define valid `HUM-*` authority assignments.
2. `aisdgs standard verify-lock` exits `0` with 100% SHA-256 digest match across all Level 1 artifacts.
3. Zero `EXC-*` records target `[NON-WAIVABLE]` rules.
4. No `APPROVED` governance record lists an AI agent profile (`AI-*`) as approver.
